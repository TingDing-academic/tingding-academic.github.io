My research focuses on **demand-side energy management**, including multi-energy coordination, demand response, energy sharing, and peer-to-peer energy trading.

**Multi-Energy Coordinated Planning of Prosumer Integrated Energy Systems Considering Source-Load Uncertainties**

Addressed integrated energy system planning under multiple source-load uncertainties by developing a risk-aware scenario generation and reduction framework integrating a conditional variational autoencoder-generative adversarial network (CVAE-GAN), Latin hypercube sampling, and Gaussian Copula, and formulated an energy-hub-based two-stage stochastic programming model for joint optimization of generation, conversion, and storage capacities and multi-energy operation.

<div class="research-section-gap" aria-hidden="true"></div>

**Multi-Time-Scale Coordinated Scheduling of Prosumer Integrated Energy Systems Considering Multi-Energy Demand Response**

Developed a day-ahead-intraday-real-time multi-time-scale scheduling framework incorporating multi-energy demand response, a second-order building thermal dynamics model, fuzzy chance constraints, thermal comfort, and ε-constraint multi-objective optimization to coordinate operating economy, energy-use satisfaction, and occupant thermal comfort.

<div class="research-section-gap" aria-hidden="true"></div>

**Risk-Averse Cooperative Operation Strategy for Prosumer Shared Energy Storage Systems Based on Asymmetric Nash Bargaining**

Proposed a risk-averse cooperative operation mechanism for shared energy storage by integrating conditional value-at-risk (CVaR), asymmetric Nash bargaining, and Shapley-value-based allocation, and employed the consensus alternating direction method of multipliers (ADMM) to enable privacy-preserving distributed optimization, improving benefit-allocation fairness and the stability of multi-stakeholder cooperation.

<div class="research-section-gap" aria-hidden="true"></div>

**Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Energy Trading Strategy for Prosumer Clusters Based on Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning**

Developed a continuous-double-auction-based local energy market for large-scale prosumer peer-to-peer (P2P) energy trading and proposed an attention-based multi-agent twin delayed deep deterministic policy gradient (TD3) algorithm under centralized training and decentralized execution with shared Actor parameters, improving training efficiency, stability, economic performance, and scalability of multi-agent trading decisions.
